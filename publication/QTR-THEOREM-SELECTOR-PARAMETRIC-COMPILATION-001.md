## 2. Selector-functional condition

Let

[

The selector-basis requirement is that the columns

[

QTR constructs and verifies the inverse column map explicitly. Given a desired functional (f), the implementation computes a selector coordinate (a) satisfying

Thus varying (z) remains within the requested class.

## 8. Theorem — selector-parametric compilation correctness

**Theorem.** Let (D) be the symbolic expression DAG obtained by:

1. constructing the symbolic local factors of Section 5;
2. eliminating every stabilizer-degeneracy variable in any fixed complete elimination order using exact semiring multiplication and marginalization;
3. multiplying any remaining scalar factors.

Let (R) be the root expression.

Then for every selector assignment (ainmathbb F_2^m),

[

}

where (F(a)) is the exact fixed-selector class contraction of Section 4.

### Proof

By Lemma 1, before any elimination step every symbolic local factor specializes to the corresponding fixed-selector numerical factor.

Apply Lemma 2 inductively after the first eliminated degeneracy variable, then the second, and so on through the complete elimination order.

After all (r) variables have been eliminated, every remaining factor is scalar. Their symbolic semiring product specializes to the product of the corresponding fixed-selector scalars.

Exact variable elimination preserves the full contraction over the eliminated variables by distributivity. Therefore the resulting numerical scalar is

[

=

]

Hence

[
operatorname{ev}_a(R)=F(a)
]

for every (a).

QED.

## 9. Lemma 3 — hash-consing and canonical operand ordering preserve denotation

The implementation interns structurally equal expression nodes and, for commutative binary operations, orders child identifiers canonically before interning.

**Lemma.** These transformations do not change (operatorname{ev}_a) for any selector (a).

### Proof

Hash-consing replaces two syntactically identical subexpressions by one shared node. The recursive denotation is unchanged.

For a commutative operation (star),

[
xstar y=ystar x.
]

Therefore swapping child order before interning preserves denotation.

The simplification

[
operatorname{ITE}(i,x,x)mapsto x
]

is also semantically exact for both selector values.

QED.

Thus canonicalization changes storage identity and sharing only, not the represented inference function.

## 12. C72 generalization

The larger symbolic compiler uses the same construction with an arbitrary selector-basis list:

`selector_parameter = {qubit: index for index, qubit in enumerate(selector_basis)}`.

For every local factor it creates exactly the same selector-conditioned terminal choice and performs the same symbolic elimination.

Therefore the theorem applies to the mathematical C72 symbolic construction whenever:

1. the selector-basis qubits are distinct;
2. the selector-functional column map has full rank;
3. the stabilizer scopes correspond to the chosen independent stabilizer basis;
4. the declared algebra operations satisfy the semiring laws used above.

The C72 work package separately records exact compiled-versus-independent-oracle equality on its frozen 300-selector validation set.

That finite validation is implementation evidence. The theorem supplies the all-selector mathematical identity for the construction, but publication must still distinguish mathematical correctness from implementation conformance.

## 14. Relationship to general knowledge compilation

The theorem is an instance of a broader known fact: exact variable-elimination traces can be compiled into arithmetic circuits, and later evidence specialization can be evaluated on the compiled circuit.

Accordingly, the theorem is not claimed as a new theorem of probabilistic inference.

Its publication role is to make precise that the QTR qLDPC construction satisfies the hypotheses and that its selector/logical/stabilizer semantics, including the min-plus product payload and QEC tie/correction rules, are preserved exactly.

The potentially publishable novelty lies in the QEC construction and its exact verified application, not in distributivity itself.
